OSCR and SSH-Breed organize AI for Open Science: Accelerating Discovery and Transparency

🎓 OSCR and SSH-Breed organize AI for Open Science: Accelerating Discovery and Transparency

📅 25 November 2025 | 11:00-13:00 | 🏫 Polak 1-20 | 🥗 Lunch Included

Join us for a joint meeting of the Open Science Community Rotterdam (OSCR) and SSH-Breed that brings together academics from diverse fields to explore how AI might benefit Open Science.

Each of our four expert speakers will present for 15 minutes, followed by a lunch at 12:00 during which you can informally discuss with the speakers. At 12:30 there is an optional workshop on the use of AI to make research more accessible.

Talks (11:00):

  • Aurelie Lemmens - Fairness Considerations When Using AI in Your Research
  • João Fernando Ferreira Gonçalves - Not so Open AI: How to Responsibly Make AI Use in Research Open and Replicable?
  • Brandon Sommer - AI, Reproducibility, and the Future of Academic Books
  • Nick Jelicic - Open Language Models for Experimental Research

    • Workshop (12:30):

  • João Fernando Ferreira Gonçalves - Using AI to Make Research More Accessible
  • Make sure to bring your laptop along

    • Whether you're just starting out or already working with open science and AI, this event is for you. This event is not only open to members of the Open Science Community Rotterdam (OSCR) or SSH-Breed. Everyone with an interest in responsible research and AI use is welcome to attend.

    Lunch will be provided at 12:00 for registered participants.

    👉 More details and registration can be found here: AI for Open Science: Accelerating Discovery and Transparency | Erasmus University Rotterdam Employees | Erasmus University Rotterdam
