# About me

Julia Reinold is a postdoctoral researcher at Erasmus University Rotterdam, specialising in high-skilled migration. She is currently working on the Horizon Europe Project Link4Skills and responsible for the design, implementation and analysis of an online survey among high- and medium-skilled migrants in the Netherlands, Germany, Poland, Austria and Canada. Julia has experience conducting quantitative, qualitative, mixed methods and experimental research to better understand migration decision-making.
